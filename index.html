Bitcoin Polymarket Tracker

A Streamlit app that collects and displays active Bitcoin-related prediction markets from Polymarket.

This project is the first building block of Eira, an AI crisis prediction system currently under development. My long-term goal is to combine prediction market data, Bitcoin market data, and other real-world signals to help forecast emerging social and economic crises.

About

Between imagination and infrastructure

“I create stories about unstable worlds—and tools that may help us understand them.”

My work moves between fiction, independent research, journalism, and software. I am especially interested in AI governance, prediction markets, crisis forecasting, experimental cities, and the political consequences of emerging technology.

I also write speculative fiction set in alternate versions of Europe, where technology, war, memory, and identity collide.
